Working in RGB vs. CMYK

All FusionX Brief Series work is HTML/CSS → PDF (digital-first). Build swatches in RGB mode, not CMYK — CMYK round-tripping causes near-black values like #050809 to drift on reimport, enough to visibly mismatch generated imagery and document backgrounds. If a piece needs offset print production, document a deliberate CMYK equivalent alongside the RGB source rather than letting software auto-convert.
